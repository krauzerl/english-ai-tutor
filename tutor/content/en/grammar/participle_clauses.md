# Zdania imiesłowowe

## Zasada
Imiesłów (-ing, III forma, having + III forma) skraca zdanie podrzędne, gdy podmiot jest ten sam. Podmiot imiesłowu musi być podmiotem zdania głównego.

## Formy
Opening the door, he saw a note. Frightened by the noise, the baby cried. Having read the instructions, Anna started the machine. The man sitting next to me is my boss.

## Poprawnie
- Opening the door, he saw a note.
- Having read the instructions, Anna started the machine.
- Frightened by the noise, the baby started to cry.
- The man sitting next to me is my boss.

## Błędnie → poprawnie
- Opening the door, a note was on the floor → Opening the door, he saw a note on the floor [morphology]
- Having tired, he went to bed → Being tired, he went to bed [morphology]
- Frightening by the noise, the baby cried → Frightened by the noise, the baby cried [morphology]
- The documents sending yesterday are here → The documents sent yesterday are here [morphology]

## Typowe błędy Polaków
- `morphology` — Wybór -ing czy III formy zależy od tego, czy podmiot sam działa, czy czegoś doświadcza.
- `word_order` — Imiesłów otwierający zdanie „wisi”, gdy nie pasuje do podmiotu zdania głównego.
