# Prośba o wyjaśnienie i powtórzenie

## Zasada
Gdy czegoś nie zrozumiałeś, poproś grzecznie: Could you say that again? Sprawdź zrozumienie przez So, you mean…?

## Formy
Could you repeat / clarify / explain…? Sorry, I didn't catch that. What do you mean by…? So, you're saying that…?

## Poprawnie
- Sorry, could you say that again, please?
- I didn't catch the last part.
- What do you mean by deadline?
- So, you mean the meeting is on Monday?

## Błędnie → poprawnie
- What do you mean by this? → Could you explain what you mean by this? [register]
- Explain me this → Could you explain this to me? [l1_calque.explain_me]
- What means deadline? → What does deadline mean? [word_order.question]
- You can repeat? → Could you repeat that? [register]

## Typowe błędy Polaków
- `l1_calque.explain_me` — Po polsku „wyjaśnij mi”; po angielsku explain wymaga to: explain it to me.
- `word_order.question` — W pytaniu potrzebny jest operator (does/could): What does it mean?
