# Zgadzanie się i niezgoda

## Zasada
Zgadzamy się wprost (I agree), niezgodę łagodzimy: I see your point, but… Po agree nie ma am: nie „I am agree”.

## Formy
I (totally) agree. I don't agree. That's a good point, but… I see what you mean, but I disagree. I'm not so sure.

## Poprawnie
- I totally agree with you.
- That's a good point, but I see it differently.
- I'm afraid I don't agree.
- I see what you mean, but I disagree.

## Błędnie → poprawnie
- I am agree with you → I agree with you [agreement.subject_verb]
- You are wrong → I'm afraid I see it differently [register]
- I agree to you → I agree with you [preposition.dependent]
- No, it is not true → I'm not sure that's true [register]

## Typowe błędy Polaków
- `agreement.subject_verb` — Polskie „jestem zgodny” bywa wnoszone do angielskiego jako I am agree.
- `register` — Zbyt bezpośrednie „You are wrong” brzmi ostro; zmiękczamy je zwrotami typu I'm afraid…
