# Codzienne czynności (słownictwo)

## Zasada
Rutynę opisujemy gotowymi zestawieniami czasownik + rzeczownik: wake up, get up, have breakfast, go to work. Uczymy się ich jako całości.

## Formy
wake up, get up, brush my teeth, have a shower (BrE) / take a shower (AmE), have breakfast, go to work, come home, watch TV, go to bed.

## Poprawnie
- I get up at seven.
- She has breakfast at eight.
- We go to work by bus.
- He goes to bed late.

## Błędnie → poprawnie
- I make a shower in the morning → I have a shower in the morning [collocation.verb_noun]
- I go to the work at eight → I go to work at eight [article.extra]
- I wake up on seven → I wake up at seven [preposition.time]
- I eat the breakfast at home → I have breakfast at home [article.extra]

## Typowe błędy Polaków
- `article.extra` — Przy work, school, bed i posiłkach nie dajemy the (go to work, have breakfast).
- `preposition.time` — Godzinę łączymy z at, nie z on ani in.

## BrE/AmE
Prysznic: have a shower (BrE), take a shower (AmE).
